Check_Balance = corrected ledger less corrected statement, the same arithmetic as Summary!D30. It is zero only when every matched item pairs one to one, so a non-zero figure means the two sides cannot both be explained by the outstanding items listed. The commonest cause is a brought-forward position older than the day before the reconciliation date, since the statements for the days in between are removed from the share each time a new batch arrives.

Stmt items and Ledger items count the outstanding lines on each side. Figures are recomputed by the robot because Summary!D20, D27 and D30 are Excel formulas that carry no stored value.
